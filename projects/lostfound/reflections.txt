On my walk to school from the subway during high-school, I would walk along St. Felix St between Hanson Pl and Lafayette Ave. Across from the Brooklyn Music School was a row of classic Brooklyn townhouses. They were mostly three or two story tall, with elevated basements and moldings on the very top. They all had cast-iron fences leading to tall, ornamented double doors, and windows along the street would be dotted with air conditioners. They were all a variety of color of a light mocha brown, ochre, and burgundy. Some of them had brick textures coming through, whereas some had a smooth texture. In the morning autumn sun, which is how I always remember that street as I head to first period health class, the rays of light basked the entire street, giving the houses an orange tint.
